Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

1. Overview

Moxie Nihongo is an offline Japanese practice application for beginners, covering grammar, particles, vocabulary, kana (hiragana and katakana) and kanji. This policy explains what information the app collects — which, in short, is none.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app:

  • has no account or sign-in system;
  • does not collect analytics, usage statistics, or crash reports;
  • contains no advertising and no tracking of any kind;
  • works entirely offline — all practice content is stored inside the app on your device;
  • does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files.

Your practice progress is stored only on your own device and is never sent anywhere.

3. Third parties

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google's own processing of data (such as account and device information collected when you install apps) is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. We do not share any information with third parties because we do not collect any.

4. Children's privacy

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Since no data is collected at all, no data about children is processed.

5. Permissions

The app does not require any sensitive Android permissions.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new effective date.
